# Agent logo sources

These local WebP copies let the campus identify its eight CLI providers in the single-file dashboard. The artwork comes from each provider's official website or application. The copies are resized and trimmed of transparent padding where present; the marks and their colors are unchanged.

These marks identify compatible products. They belong to their respective owners, are not offered under the repository's MIT license, and do not indicate endorsement or affiliation.
